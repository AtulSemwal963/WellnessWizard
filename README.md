Wellness Wizard:
AI-powered mobile app for personalized symptom analysis, wellness tracking, and actionable health insights — making reliable healthcare accessible to all.

📱 About Wellness Wizard:
Wellness Wizard is an AI-driven health companion designed to bridge the gap between individuals and reliable healthcare.
Our app delivers:

Personalized Symptom Analysis — AI evaluates user-reported symptoms and provides clinically-backed guidance.

Actionable Health Insights — Tailored recommendations based on medical history and lifestyle.

Wellness Tracking — Monitor physical and mental health trends over time for better self-management.

We aim to empower users, especially the underserved, with accessible, affordable, and trustworthy health information — anytime, anywhere.

✨ Key Features:
AI-Powered Symptom Checker

Custom Wellness Plans & Recommendations

Personalized Tracking Dashboards

Secure and Private Data Handling

Accessibility Focus for Seniors and Remote Areas
